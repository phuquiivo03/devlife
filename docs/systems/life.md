# Life sim (M5)

Time slots, vitals, bills, small events, the Thông báo panel, the store, and personality. The spec is [docs/specs/M5_life_sim.md](../specs/M5_life_sim.md). Trait icons in `public/ui/m5/` are the approved 16×16 sheets for the spec keys. The character card uses the matching 32×32 `_x2` file at 22px. The other M5 art there is the approved native PNG, including store furniture placed from `src/game/life/placement.json`. The life-sim one-shots are in the SFX sprite. There is no personality sound. One moment plays one life cue. A specific cue replaces the severity ping, and the same cue fired twice in one tick plays once. A choice or a buff plays `sfx_event_open`. Salary plays `sfx_salary`. The sick threshold plays `sfx_sick_cough`. The day-7 landlord knock plays `sfx_landlord_knock`. The important-day banner shares that same tick and plays `sfx_event_open`. Sleep plays `sfx_diary_flip`. A successful purchase plays `sfx_store_buy` and the existing money-spend cue.

Numbers live in [config/life_sim.json](../../config/life_sim.json), [config/store.json](../../config/store.json), and [config/traits.json](../../config/traits.json). Starting money stays in `config/game_config.json` (`3000000`).

## Calendar

Day 1 is Monday. A month is 28 days, a year is 12 months. One slot is 2 hours. A weekday budget is 10 slots from 06:00 (through 02:00). A weekend budget is 12 (through 06:00). The budget is captured at 06:00 so midnight does not change it.

Staying into a slot past that budget costs 4 health immediately and queues −5 mood for the next 06:00.

Dated holidays: Tết (month 1, days 1–3), 30/4 as the last day of month 4, 1/5 as month 5 day 1, Trung thu month 8 day 15, Black Friday month 11 day 26 (the 4th Friday when day 1 is Monday). Finals and salary review have no dates yet, so they are not on the calendar. Friday is a deploy day, used by the hackathon. Holiday banners do not change stats. Monday still costs the morning mood.

## Stats

Energy, health, and mood are floats from 0 to 100. The HUD rounds them. An action applies, in order: base, diminishing on a positive mood (`× 0.6^times today`, reset at 06:00), the generic personality multiplier `× 1`, furniture, then a work-streak energy cost `× 1.2` when the streak is at least 3, then clamp, then thresholds. Named traits apply their own rates on top of that, listed below. An event effect adds and clamps. It skips trait multipliers, furniture, diminishing, and the work streak. The Lạc quan mood floor of 20 still applies to that path. At most 3 furniture contributions apply to one stat; the largest are kept.

Each waking slot: mood −1, times the trait decay (1.3 for Chăm chỉ, otherwise 1). If the work streak is at least 3, another −2, not multiplied again.

End of the calendar day (midnight, or sleep if the player skipped midnight): fewer than 2 meals costs 5 health, exactly 3 meals restores 2. Owned furniture then adds its daily health and mood. Mood moves 10% of the way toward 60. Three work days in a row with fewer than 2 fun slots costs 3 mood. A finished course or a finished job counts as work. This phase has no fun action, so fun slots stay 0.

Sleep of `h` hours: energy max is 70 while health is under 30, otherwise 100. Energy becomes `min(max, energy + 12.5h + sleep bonus)`. Under 6 hours, health loses `4 × (6 − h)` and the next morning loses 5 mood.

Thresholds: energy under 15 blocks taking a job. Energy under an action's cost blocks it; **Cố làm** spends 5 health and does it anyway (not available under the 15 floor). Mood under 30 multiplies XP by 0.7 (floored) and adds 1 bug. Mood under 15 schedules a freelance lock for the next day. Health under 10 is a hospital stay: health returns to 10, money −1,000,000, the clock jumps 2 days to 06:00, and those skipped days do not run the meal check.

## Bills

Rent and utilities are due on day 1 of a month. Salary is paid on day 10: Junior 15,000,000, Senior 45,000,000 (the low end of the spec ranges). Student has no salary. The opening day does not emit `dayStarted`, so the first charge is the next month's day 1.

If rent does not clear, each day of that unpaid month costs 3 mood and increments the debt counter. Debt day 3 opens “Chủ trọ nhắc khéo tiền nhà.” Debt day 7 opens “Chủ trọ gõ cửa.” Those two dialogs do not use the daily small-event slot and do not reset pity. **Trả trọ** pays the outstanding rent when the player can afford it; otherwise the dialog stays open. Utilities that do not clear are skipped. Paying a later month's rent clears the counter.

Food, from the character panel: mì 8,000 (health −3), cơm nhà 25,000, cơm tấm 35,000. Three meals a day. Freelance payout is `base × (1 + 0.1 × tier) × (0.8 + reputation / 250)`, rounded. `base` is the template pay.

## Events

One small event can fire per day, at the end of a slot from 10:00 onward. Chance is `0.12 + min(pity, 3) × 0.05`. An empty day adds 1 pity, capped at 3. A fired event resets pity to 0 and cools that event down for 5 days. Mood under 30 multiplies a good event's weight by 1.5. Effects add and clamp. Event XP uses the same mood ×0.7 floor as job XP. A lost slot jumps the clock 2 hours without another roll. While the task terminal is open, the roll waits and flushes when the terminal closes.

Choice copy is the one-line spec text. Đồ án is student only. Prod, họp, headhunter, and đồng nghiệp are junior and senior. Karaoke and mất điện are evening and night. Hackathon is Friday only and resolves the Monday after: win chance `0.10 + 0.03 × max skill level`, prize 20,000,000 and 200 XP on the highest skill. “Mở store” opens the cửa hàng.

A pending event draws on the lower part of the room canvas, not along the bottom of the page. The card is 424×186 CSS pixels and scales down, keeping that ratio, on a narrower or shorter canvas. There is no dimmed backdrop, so the room, the HUD, and the panels stay visible and keep their clicks. The card fades in and rises 4px; `prefers-reduced-motion` skips that. Its layer is above the canvas and the side panels and below the holiday banner, modals, and tooltips. Portraits, lines, and choices are unchanged. Those numbers are `LIFE_EVENT_POPUP` in `src/components/game/life-event-popup.config.ts` and [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](../design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

## Store

Rooms and furniture are bought from **Cửa hàng**, the shop icon on the room status bar above the room. Đi ngủ stays on the HUD card. The list opens in a modal as a grid: 4 columns from 1024px and 2 below that, with tabs Nội thất, Phòng, and Tất cả. Column counts are `STORE_GRID`. Junior is 25,000,000 and needs one skill at level 3 plus 30 reputation. Senior is 180,000,000 and needs two skills at level 8. The header, the 1/2/3 keys, and `?stage=` stay on a room until it is bought. Student is the start and stays available. Buying a room moves the player there. Rent, utilities, and salary follow that room.

A room's sleep, mood, and health bonuses apply only while the player lives in it. A bought item's bonus applies in every room. The plant is +2 mood a day. The fan and air conditioner add 5 and 10 sleep energy. The mattress is +1 health a day. The ergo chair multiplies job accept and retry energy by 0.9 before the work streak. The second monitor multiplies study and job XP by 1.1, floored, before the mood multiplier, and it stays locked until the Junior room is owned. Event XP is unchanged.

Black Friday multiplies prices by 0.9. One pinned item shows `label percent%` on the sleep diary, using that day's price. When money covers the pin, one notice says “Đủ tiền …”. Floor sprites replace `student.mattress`, `junior.office_chair`, and `junior.bed` and draw their shadow under the placement anchor. `item_mattress.png` is not used.

A purchase grants `purchase_mood` (4). Buying a room grants `room_purchase_mood` (10). Both go through the same diminishing returns as other positive mood. Tiết kiệm halves the grant first, so those amounts are 2 and 5.

## Personality

A new character rolls exactly one trait and stores `traitRolled`. The creation popup spins the six trait icons for 1.5 seconds, flashes the result, then shows the avatar, the effect, and the starting stats in two rows (Sức khỏe, Tinh thần, Năng lượng, Tiền) until **Bắt đầu** on the shared pixel button. The character card draws that 32×32 sheet at 22px, next to the Vietnamese name. The bonus line is a tooltip (`src/components/ui/tooltip.config.ts`). Older saves are marked seen and skip that popup. Older saves roll one trait once during migration. The flag keeps that roll. A version 11 save whose trait is already one of the other five keeps it and is marked rolled. A version 11 Cẩn thận is rolled once.

Cú đêm: study and job XP granted while the clock is at 22:00 or later, or before 06:00, is ×1.2, floored. The clock is the minute when the XP is granted. At 06:00, energy −10. Chăm chỉ: study XP ×1.15, floored, and the slot mood decay is ×1.3. Hướng ngoại: soft-skill study and job XP ×1.25, floored, and freelance pay ×1.05 after the pay formula, rounded. Monthly salary is unchanged. A day with no fun slot still counts as home. `extrovertHomePenalty` is false, so that run of home days costs no mood. The soft-skill XP and the pay bonus stay. Tiết kiệm: food prices ×0.8, rounded. The button shows the price that is charged. A positive mood from a store purchase is ×0.5. Cẩn thận: the bug count loses 1 and stays inside the formula min and max. Accepted jobs store hours ×1.1, and the task terminal's ticket, build, and test durations are ×1.1. Lạc quan: mood from every source, including events, does not go under 20. Study and job XP ×0.95, floored. Trait XP is applied before the monitor's ×1.1, and mood ×0.7 is applied after both. Mood under 30 still multiplies XP by 0.7, so a floor of 20 keeps that penalty. Event XP, including the hackathon, skips the trait XP rates.

## Thông báo and UX

The left column is the notice list, newest first. Unread rows show `ui_badge_unread.png`. Empty copy is “Chưa có thông báo.” A new row plays `sfx_notif_info`, `sfx_notif_warn`, or `sfx_notif_urgent`, unless that row carries a specific cue. Payday plays `sfx_salary`. Health under `sick_below` plays `sfx_sick_cough` once. That row carries action `sleep`, which calls the same sleep control as the HUD bed (it runs only while sleep is allowed). The day-7 landlord plays `sfx_landlord_knock`. A choice or a buff plays only `sfx_event_open` for that moment. Warn and urgent lines also print `> WARN:` in the task terminal. That line blinks amber three times and shakes lightly, the same treatment as a task WARNING. Stat changes float on the HUD bars. Health under 30, mood under 15, and energy under 15 blink, and the room shows cough, cloud, or sweat. A holiday banner uses `ui_banner_v2` for 3 seconds. The sheet is drawn at 2× integer scale, centered, and overlays both side panels. It slides down and bounces, a gold edge sparkles, and many copies of `fx_confetti` fall for 1.5 seconds. A light dim covers the screen. The words are drawn by the game in the center box from `slices.json` (x 40–440, y 10–46 on the native sheet). It plays `sfx_event_open` as part of the same moment as that morning's notices. Sleep opens the diary (`sfx_diary_flip`) as a notepad window titled `nhat_ky_ngay_N.txt`, then the snapshot is recaptured and the day's purse is cleared. The first line is the night that just ended (Ngày lễ when that day is a holiday, the weekday, and ngủ lúc the clock they slept). Kiếm được is green. Đã tiêu is red, with Ăn, Trọ, and Mua đồ on one short line when those spends happened. Còn lại follows, then one line for XP, sức khỏe, tinh thần, and năng lượng. That flip is the only sound for the moment.

## Meals

Meals track waking slots since the last one. 0–2 is No, 3–4 is Hơi đói, and 5 or more is Đói. The rice-bowl button (`HungerDock`, a `PixelButton` icon with `icon_btn_food` at 2× inside the same box as Đi ngủ) sits on the HUD icon bar immediately before Đi ngủ and opens the food list in the shared modal. The bowl icon itself pulses when the player is not full. Hơi đói is a slow, faint opacity and brightness dip (`HUNGER_BOWL_BLINK.peckish`, 2200ms, opacity down to 0.78, brightness 0.94). Đói is faster and clearly stronger (`HUNGER_BOWL_BLINK.hungry`, 700ms, opacity down to 0.45, brightness 0.7). No stays still. `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the pulse and holds a static tint (Hơi đói opacity 0.9 / brightness 0.96, Đói opacity 0.62 / brightness 0.78). Those numbers live in `src/components/game/food-dock.config.ts`. The shared tooltip (`src/components/ui/tooltip.tsx`) and the button `aria-label` read “Ăn · ” plus the level, for example “Ăn · Hơi đói”. The character panel does not list meals. Đói pushes one “Đói rồi, đi ăn thôi.” notice through the existing notice list. That row carries action `eat`, which opens this same food modal. Eating sets the slot count back to 0. Thresholds are `hunger_full_until` and `hunger_peckish_until`.

## Save

Persist version 16. Version 16 adds `sleptDay` and `sleptAt` on the open diary and leaves both empty for an older diary. Version 15 adds `life.slotsSinceMeal` and starts an older save at 0. Version 14 adds `feed.purse` and clears it for an older save, so the next diary still keeps that save's money. Version 13 adds `players.introSeen`. A save from before that keeps its trait roll and skips the creation popup. A version 11 save whose trait is already one of the other five keeps that trait and is marked rolled. Home days from version 11 stay. A save from version 9 or 10 keeps its money, skills, shop, room, and notice cues, starts home days at 0, and receives one rolled trait. A notice may carry `cue`; a save from version 8 keeps its money, skills, shop, and progress, and its notices get `cue` null. Older saves keep money and energy and gain health 70, mood 70, empty life counters, and an empty event feed. A save from before the store owns nothing and wakes in the student room. Junior and senior rooms are bought in the store. The standing migration rule is [docs/systems/save.md](save.md).
